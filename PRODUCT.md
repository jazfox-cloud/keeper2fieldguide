## Stack

Astro, consumed from the verified neutral `game-guide-starter` baseline at `ecfd7ff71f41c6c753b5850b08002a388c9614e7`. The user explicitly approved reusing the existing website template and skills. The site is deployed through the GitHub-backed Cloudflare Pages project `keeper2fieldguide` from the repository `jazfox-cloud/keeper2fieldguide`.

## Users

People searching before launch for a concise, source-backed answer about Graveyard Keeper 2: release timing, official platforms, current single-player status, Windows requirements, and confirmed gameplay systems. They need to distinguish what official sources currently confirm from what cannot be verified until the released build is available.

## Product Purpose

Keeper 2 Field Guide is an independent English-language guide that routes each pre-launch search task to one clear answer page. Success means a visitor can get the current answer quickly, inspect the official evidence and verification date, and see uncertainty instead of fabricated depth.

## Positioning

The site publishes only page scopes that current official evidence can answer completely, while holding recipes, maps, progression, quests, and other release-dependent guides until reproducible first-hand evidence exists.

## Operating Context

The initial site has six search pages: `/`, `/release-date/`, `/system-requirements/`, `/platforms/`, `/multiplayer/`, and `/gameplay/`. Every page is maintained against typed source and fact records. Official storefront and developer changes trigger freshness review; search-signal demand never substitutes for factual evidence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- English only for Sprint 002.
- Static evidence-first guide; no account, database, map, calculator, ads, localization, or analytics.
- Official tinyBuild video and approved official media-kit images may be used only with their recorded source/public-use metadata, visible attribution, accessibility metadata, and evidence-scope limits.
- The public search surface is exactly six approved pages. Privacy, terms, and 404 are structural routes and not search-demand pages.
- `keeper2fieldguide.com` is the active canonical production domain. Repository creation, GitHub-backed Pages deployment, root DNS binding, and the public sitemap are complete; GA4, Search Console verification/submission, and future indexing actions remain separately approval-gated.
- Current facts are pre-release facts verified on 2026-09-01. Exact recipes, progression, maps, quest steps, NPC schedules, and post-launch mechanics are unresolved and must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Independent publisher identity: `Keeper 2 Field Guide`.
- Public game-page H1 may use the full game name where search clarity requires it.
- Owned text wordmark and favicon; never use the official game logo as the publisher mark.
- Restrained charcoal, muted violet, and warm parchment/ochre palette without copying official trade dress.
- Visible independent/unofficial disclaimer and clearly labeled official destinations.
- Direct, calm, evidence-aware English copy. No hype, false completeness, fake ratings, fake offers, or implied affiliation.

## Product Principles

1. One distinct user task owns one stable URL.
2. Official or first-hand evidence owns public facts; search and community data own discovery only.
3. Show verification dates and useful unknowns at the point of decision.
4. Strengthen an existing owner page before creating a new URL.
5. Keep local build proof, release approval, deployment, and measurement as separate gates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast for body text, keep keyboard-visible focus and a working skip link, expose current navigation state, preserve one H1 and semantic heading order per page, use accessible table headings with contained small-screen scrolling, maintain at least 44 px tap targets, keep the video embed responsive, and prevent horizontal page overflow from 320 px upward.
